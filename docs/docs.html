Getting Started

Welcome to the Comfort Zone Platform. This guide will walk you through setting up your organization from scratch. The entire process takes less than 10 minutes.

1

Create Your Account

Visit the registration page and fill in your name, email, phone number, and password. Choose your organization type (portal) from the 15 available options. Your organization name and subdomain will be created automatically.

2

Verify Your Email

Check your email inbox for a verification link. Click the link to verify your account. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder or request a new verification email from the login page.

3

Complete Your Profile

After logging in, complete your organization profile including logo upload, address, phone number, and other details. This information appears on reports, invoices, and other generated documents.

4

Configure Modules

Navigate to the Settings page to enable and configure the modules relevant to your organization type. Each portal type comes with pre-configured modules, but you can customize everything to match your workflow.

5

Invite Team Members

Go to User Management and invite your staff. Assign roles (admin, manager, staff, viewer) to control what each person can access. Each user receives an email invitation to join your organization.

Need help? Use the in-app AI assistant to ask questions in plain language. Try "How do I add a new student?" or "Show me fee collection report."

Choosing Your Portal Type

The Comfort Zone Platform supports 15 different organization types, each with pre-built modules tailored to that industry. When you register, you select your primary portal type. Here's a quick overview:

  • School: Full education management — enrollment, fees, exams, attendance, LMS, parent portal, library, transport, hostel
  • Church: Member management, tithe tracking, sermon library, events, prayer requests, groups, fundraising
  • Clinic / Hospital: Patient records, appointments, prescriptions, lab, pharmacy, departments, wards, billing, referrals
  • Business / ERP: POS, inventory, payroll, CRM, invoicing, expenses, multi-branch, financial reporting
  • Hotel: Room management, bookings, check-in/check-out, guest services, housekeeping
  • Restaurant: Menu management, orders, kitchen display, table management, recipes
  • Retail / Shop: Sales, stock control, suppliers, customer loyalty, barcode scanning
  • Salon / Spa: Appointment scheduling, service catalog, staff management, payments
  • Pharmacy: Drug inventory, dispensing, expiry tracking, prescription management
  • Gym / Fitness: Memberships, class scheduling, trainer management, attendance
  • Hardware Store: Product catalog, pricing, quotations, supplier management
  • Supermarket: POS terminals, barcode scanning, stock control, promotions
  • Transport: Fleet management, routes, bookings, tracking, driver management
  • Electronics: Product catalog, repairs, warranties, supplier management
  • Individual: Personal finance, task management, notes, goals
Not sure? You can switch portal types at any time from the Settings page. Your data is preserved during the switch, and you'll gain access to the modules for your new portal type.

First Login

After registering and verifying your email, log in at ssewasswa.onrender.com/login. You'll be taken directly to your portal dashboard. The dashboard shows key metrics, recent activity, quick actions, and notifications specific to your portal type.

Take a moment to explore the navigation menu on the left side. The menu adapts to your portal type, showing only the modules relevant to your organization. Use the search bar at the top to quickly find any feature or record.

School Setup Guide

1. Add Classes and Streams

Navigate to Academics > Classes. Create all your classes (e.g., P1 through P7, S1 through S6) with their streams (e.g., North, South, East, West). Set the class teacher for each stream.

2. Register Students

Go to Students > Add Student. Fill in student details including name, date of birth, gender, parent/guardian information, and photo. Assign to a class and stream. The system generates an admission number automatically.

3. Set Up Fee Structures

Navigate to Finance > Fee Structure. Define fees for each class by term. Include tuition, development fees, lunch, transport, and any other charges. Set payment deadlines and installment options.

4. Configure Subjects and Teachers

Go to Academics > Subjects to create your subject catalog. Then assign subjects to classes and teachers. Use the Timetable Builder to create the weekly schedule automatically or manually.

5. Enable Parent Portal

Parent accounts are created automatically when you add student parent details. Parents can log in to view attendance, grades, fee balances, and receive notifications. Send them their login credentials via SMS.

Church Setup Guide

1. Add Members

Go to Members > Add Member. Enter personal details, family information, membership date, and status (active, inactive, visitor). Add family relationships to connect members.

2. Configure Giving Categories

Navigate to Finance > Categories. Create categories for tithes, offerings (general, thanksgiving, special), donations, and any other income types your church tracks.

3. Set Up Services

Go to Services > Service Schedule. Define your regular services (Sunday morning, evening, mid-week, youth). Assign speakers, worship leaders, and other roles.

Clinic Setup Guide

1. Add Departments

Navigate to Administration > Departments. Create departments like OPD, IPD, Laboratory, Pharmacy, Maternity, Pediatrics, and Surgery. Assign department heads and staff.

2. Register Staff

Go to Staff > Add Staff. Enter doctor, nurse, pharmacist, lab technician, and receptionist details. Set their specialties, departments, and consultation schedules.

3. Configure Services and Pricing

Navigate to Services > Service Catalog. Define consultation fees, lab test prices, procedure costs, and room charges. Set different rates for insurance and cash patients.

Business Setup Guide

1. Add Products or Services

Go to Inventory > Products. Add your complete product catalog with prices, categories, barcode/SKU numbers, and stock levels. For service businesses, create service items instead.

2. Set Up Payment Methods

Navigate to Settings > Payments. Configure your accepted payment methods: cash, mobile money (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money), card (DPO, Flutterwave), and bank transfer.

3. Configure Tax Settings

Set up VAT rates, tax categories, and automatic tax calculations on invoices. Configure URA (Uganda Revenue Authority) compliant tax reporting.

Switching Portal Types

You can change your organization's portal type at any time. Click the purple "Portal" button in the navigation bar, or go to Settings > Switch Portal. Select your new portal type and confirm. The switch takes effect immediately — you'll see the new dashboard with modules for your chosen type.

Your existing data is preserved during the switch. However, some module-specific data may not be accessible until you switch back to the original portal type. Shared modules (users, settings, communications) remain fully accessible.

Payment Setup

The Comfort Zone Platform supports multiple payment methods commonly used in Africa:

  • MTN Mobile Money: Requires MTN MoMo Merchant API credentials. Contact your MTN business representative to get set up.
  • Airtel Money: Requires Airtel Money Merchant API credentials. Available for Uganda, Kenya, and other markets.
  • Card Payments: Integrated with DPO (Direct Pay Online) and Flutterwave. Supports Visa, Mastercard, and local cards.
  • Cash: Always available as a manual payment option.

Navigate to Settings > Payments to configure each method. Payment credentials are encrypted and stored securely.

User Management

Go to Settings > Users to manage your team. Each user has a role that determines their access level:

  • Super Admin: Full access to everything including system settings and user management.
  • Admin: Access to most modules. Can manage data but not system configuration.
  • Manager: Access to assigned modules. Can view reports and manage records.
  • Staff: Basic access for data entry and daily operations.
  • Viewer: Read-only access to dashboards and reports.

Offline Mode

The Comfort Zone Platform supports offline operation. When your internet connection drops, you can continue using the platform normally. All changes are stored locally and synced automatically when the connection is restored. No data is lost.

Offline mode works for all core operations: data entry, viewing records, generating reports, and more. Some features that require real-time connectivity (payments, SMS, notifications) will queue and execute when back online.

API Access

The platform provides a RESTful API for integration with external systems. API keys can be generated from Settings > API. The API supports standard CRUD operations on all data entities, plus webhooks for real-time event notifications.

API documentation with endpoints, authentication, request/response formats, and examples is available through the in-app developer console.
