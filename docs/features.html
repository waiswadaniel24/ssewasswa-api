Platform Features

70+ modules across 15 portal types. Every feature is designed to solve real problems for African organizations.

School Management

Student Enrollment

Complete student registration with photo uploads, parent/guardian details, class assignment, and admission number auto-generation.

Fee Management

Define fee structures per class and term. Track payments, balances, and generate statements. Integration with MTN MoMo and Airtel Money for instant collection.

Gradebook & Report Cards

Enter marks by subject and exam. Auto-calculate totals, averages, grades, and positions. Generate PDF and Word report cards with school branding.

Attendance Tracking

Daily attendance via QR code scan, manual entry, or biometric. Real-time notifications to parents when students are absent. Weekly and monthly reports.

Timetable Builder

Drag-and-drop timetable creation. Handle teacher conflicts, room allocation, and subject distribution. Print or share timetables with staff and students.

Learning Management System

Upload lessons, assignments, and quizzes. Students access materials online. Auto-grading for multiple choice questions. Teacher-student messaging.

Parent Portal

Parents view attendance, grades, fee balances, and school announcements in real-time. Receive push notifications and WhatsApp messages for important updates.

Exam Management

Create exam schedules, assign supervisors, manage exam rooms. Generate seating plans and mark entry sheets. Automated results processing and analysis.

Library System

Catalog books, manage lending and returns, track overdue items, calculate fines. Students browse the library catalog online and reserve books.

Promotions & Graduation

Automated promotion recommendations based on performance criteria. Graduation tracking with certificate generation. Academic year management.

Transport & Hostel

Manage school buses with route planning and GPS tracking. Hostel room allocation, bed management, visitor logs, and welfare monitoring.

Discipline & Behavior

Record incidents, warnings, and disciplinary actions. Track behavior patterns over time. Automatic parent notifications for serious incidents.

Member Directory

Tithe & Offering Tracking

Record tithes, offerings, and donations with receipt generation. Member giving statements. Trend analysis and reporting by period and category.

Sermon Library

Upload and organize sermons by series, topic, or date. Audio and video support. Members browse and listen online. Search by scripture reference.

Event Management

Plan services, conferences, and special events. Manage registrations, venues, schedules. Send invitations and reminders via SMS and email.

Group Ministries

Manage cell groups, youth, women's, men's, and children's ministries. Group attendance tracking, meeting schedules, and member assignments.

Prayer Requests

Submit and track prayer requests. Assign to prayer teams. Mark as answered and share testimonies. Privacy controls for sensitive requests.

Fundraising

Plan and track fundraising campaigns. Peer-to-peer fundraising with personal links. Progress dashboards and donor acknowledgment.

Visitor Follow-Up

Log visitor information from services. Auto-assign follow-up contacts. Track follow-up status and convert visitors to members.

Patient Registry

Appointments & Queue

Consultations & Prescriptions

Laboratory & Diagnostics

Lab test requests and result entry. Test catalog management. Result tracking and notification. Integration with consultation workflow.

Pharmacy & Dispensing

Drug inventory with expiry tracking. Prescription dispensing with dosage instructions. Stock alerts, batch management, and supplier orders.

Department Management

Manage hospital departments including OPD, IPD, maternity, pediatrics, surgery. Department-level analytics and resource allocation.

Billing & Insurance

Service billing with insurance claim processing. Multiple payment methods including mobile money. Invoice generation and receipt printing.

Vitals Tracking

Record and chart vital signs: blood pressure, temperature, weight, height, BMI. Trend analysis over time. Abnormal value alerts.

Referrals

Create and track patient referrals to specialists or external facilities. Referral status management. Referral network directory.

Health Analytics

Dashboards showing patient demographics, common diagnoses, revenue trends, and resource utilization. Disease surveillance and outbreak detection.

Point of Sale (POS)

Fast checkout with barcode scanning, product search, and quick keys. Support for cash, card, and mobile money. Receipt printing and email/SMS delivery.

Inventory Management

Multi-branch stock control with real-time levels. Automatic low-stock alerts. Batch tracking, serial numbers, and expiry management. Stock transfers between branches.

Payroll & HR

Employee records, salary structures, tax calculations (PAYE, NSSF). Payslip generation. Leave management, overtime tracking, and loan deductions.

Customer Management (CRM)

Customer profiles with purchase history. Loyalty points and rewards. Customer segmentation and targeted communication via SMS and email.

Invoicing & Quotations

Financial Reporting

Profit and loss statements, balance sheets, cash flow reports. Daily, weekly, monthly, and custom date range reports. Export to PDF and Excel.

Expense Tracking

Record and categorize business expenses. Receipt photo uploads. Budget vs actual comparisons. Approval workflows for large expenses.

Multi-Branch Support

Manage multiple locations from one dashboard. Branch-level reporting and permissions. Inter-branch stock transfers and consolidated financial reports.

Payments & Billing

MTN Mobile Money

Direct integration with MTN MoMo Collection API. Instant payment confirmation. Automatic receipt delivery via SMS and WhatsApp.

Airtel Money

Full Airtel Money merchant integration. Supports all Airtel Money transaction types. Real-time balance checks and reconciliation.

Card Payments

DPO Direct Pay Online and Flutterwave integration for Visa, Mastercard, and local card payments. Secure tokenization and recurring billing.

QR Code Payments

Generate QR codes for instant payments. Customers scan with their phone to pay. Auto-reconciliation with transaction records.

Fee Installments

Flexible installment plans from 2 to 12 months. Automated payment reminders. Track installment progress and outstanding balances.

Real-Time Dashboards

Live-updating charts and KPI panels. Revenue, attendance, enrollment, and performance metrics. Drill-down into specific departments or periods.

AI Assistant

Natural language queries across all your data. Ask questions like "How many students owe fees?" or "Show me this month's revenue." Get instant answers.
