Frequently Asked Questions

Is there really a free plan?

Yes, the Free plan is free forever. It supports up to 100 records and 5 staff users, which is perfect for small schools, churches, clinics, and businesses that are just getting started. No credit card required.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the Settings page. When upgrading, the new features are available immediately. When downgrading, your data is preserved but some premium features become restricted.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, bank transfer, and card payments (via DPO and Flutterwave). You can also pay via Flutterwave for international payments.

Can I change my portal type?

Yes, you can switch between all 15 portal types at any time at no extra cost. Go to Settings > Switch Portal to change your organization type. Your data is preserved during the switch.

Do you offer discounts for NGOs or community organizations?

Yes, we offer special pricing for registered NGOs, CBOs, and community organizations. Contact us for details and we'll work out a plan that fits your budget.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data remains accessible in read-only mode for 90 days after cancellation. You can export everything (students, members, reports, financial records) during this period. After 90 days, data is permanently deleted from our servers.
